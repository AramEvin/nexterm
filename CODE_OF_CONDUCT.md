# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NexTerm to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience,
background, identity or the way they work. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for users and the project.
- Help newcomers: everyone started somewhere.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews)
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by contacting [@AramEvin](https://github.com/AramEvin). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour violates this code.
